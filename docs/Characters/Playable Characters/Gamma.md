## Personality

Gamma’s this sorta hyperactive, one extreme to the next, all or nothing sort of thinker. Not so insane as everyone else, surprisingly, and has the most amount of compassion for other people than anyone else. On the outside, like at work, she’s this crazy trigger happy mercenary who kills as she pleases. She completely disregards her own health and defenses, which make a lot of people think she is suicidal. She takes great joy in innards in general, but she’s at least very good at her job. 

But surprisingly, she’d do anything for her friends. She’s quite gullible and brash and loves like a dog— complete and unconditional love. She’s extremely quickly paced and switches very drastically between her emotions, but still prioritizes everyone else above herself. I’m sure you know where this goes.

This actually stresses her out greatly and she has essentially trained herself to enjoy the pain which is how she gets out her stress— gore. Gore gore and more gore. Gamma will fight until the day she dies. In fact, that’s how she hopes to go out. As an explosion, rather than a long drawn-out flame.

Gamma was born into a 16-year-old-female-titanium skeleton within generation 3 ( even though [Honeycakes](Honeycakes.md) remains to be ~10 years older than her), and is the second youngest clone in that generation outside of [Epsilon](../NPCs/Other clones/Epsilon.md). She was born prematurely, but is still quite healthy.

She never particularly stood out when fighting for Pyrrhon, except until when she’d left FUTURE. Everyone else kind of ignored the newer g3 clones, and many (like [Phi](../NPCs/Other clones/Phi.md), [Chi](Chi.md), and [Muse](../NPCs/Other clones/Muse.md)) didn’t even know that they existed.

# Gameplay

*Gamma's play style is very heavily inspired by [Akane](https://youtu.be/rvnPUK1tWWs?si=4bh3RHIZLUZOMaP4)* 

**Role**: Glass cannon.
> Essentially, character that can deal a lot of destruction, but is highly vulnerable to attacks. Hence a “cannon”, because they're so powerful, and “glass” because they're fragile, if struck. Gamma’s fast to make up for it— fast enough to outrun her enemies— if you know how to use her correctly.


**Signature Ability**: SuperSonix
> 	She can dash through enemies and projectiles without taking damage (basically she evades attack when dashing) BUT it has a 3 second cooldown. she will also damage anything within the radius of her dash.`

> 	Basically with this you can go behind enemies and attack them from behind. Each upgrade will add another benefit to her damage

**Extended Ability**: Adrenaline Junkie
> 	For each enemy killed within 3 seconds of each other, gain +1 stack of **Epinephrine**. Each stack of **Epinephrine** grants +5% ATK spd and MOV spd. 

> 	For each 1.5 second with no damage dealt, loose a stack of **Epinephrine**. 

> 	When activating SHIFT, consume all stacks of **Epinephrine** to release a shockwave which knocks enemies back.

**Base**: Fists
> 	Bare bones fist attacks! She has two quick jabs, then a final upswing, which she can then use to attack them again or use an item. The upswing will gain some magic damage on occasion.

> 		there is a way to anim cancel the third attack to get

> 			- 2 jab

> 			- Supersonix (get behind the enemy)

> 			- full 3-hit combo

> 			- Gun attack

> 			Which will result in launching the enemy quite far.

**Shift**: Gunning 
> 	When pressing SHIFT, Gamma can consume some MP to shoot bullets in the direction of wherever the mouse is. She can shoot quite a bit of bullets before she runs out of MP (And it regenerates over time, anyway). However, their range is limited and she remains stationary during this time.


**ULTIMATE**: SLAUGHTERHOUSE
>	Goes off of HP. Your health decreases by 1% + base ATK*.25 / 100`

	Time stops, leaving behind a neon trail so it gets hard to see after a while. When finished, she takes a little burst of damage.

	For each enemy killed, she gains +1% health. This number increases if she kills a lot in a combo (ie 10 enemies -> 2%…). If she dies in this state, Gamma will explode! Will instantly kill any enemy below 60% health and below miniboss status (bosses get cut down to 250% of her ATK).
