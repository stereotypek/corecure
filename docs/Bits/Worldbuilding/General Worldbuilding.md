## Vegetables
in CORE, vegetables are typically served dehydrated and dried. 
The process goes: flatten vegetables (typically herbs like parsley and mints) -> extract water -> sell each separately
The dried vegetables lack most of the nutritional content, which is why the extracted water is typically sold  at a markup. The water is expensive and popular amongst young children and adults, for being a 'revitalizing golden liquor' (as an ad will tell you). it's kind of like a healthy Celsius in the culture. It is usually too expensive for common folks (notably Constantine) to access it but Nea, who grew up generally wealthy, drank it on the daily.
