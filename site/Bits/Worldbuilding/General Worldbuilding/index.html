DOMES

Recall: Most of the ground consists of metal plates and various supports, which were then coated with concrete and then built upon. So when Gamma and Honey touch real, non-sheeted ground, they have a difficult time adjusting. The reason this was done was because some areas had disease and were completely uninhabitable, so they just built over it There are some areas, independent of Pyrrhon's reign, which still exists in the outskirts of abandoned cities and No Man's Land. However, due to the close proximimty to NML, these areas are often subject to extreme radiation, or so the news says. Places with extreme amounts of radiation-- whether built naturally or sent out separately-- are walled off in DOMES which are visible from space. air supply is pumped in outside. All those who lived there originally cannot leave, and are stuck there forever. The idea is once the population is >100, there is no reason to dedicate resources to it and air supply ceases.

Some DOMES though have been around for many, many years. And some are equipped with special filtering which should send the excess radiation buildup into space. Some bodies have fully evolved and are immune to the small amounts of radiation which are in the area, and have evolved around it. If you or a loved one want to ever go there, you can rent a proxy-body service. By remotely accessing a GI early war clone, you can see through their eyes and walk around. Of course, your trip is monitored.. But you can talk with, and hug your family. If you don't mind the smell, you can wear a bodysuit which will translate feelings experienced by the proxy body, by tightening segments of the bodysuit to emulate feelings like being held. While often not intended for sexual purpose, it is occasionally, for those who have lost their partners to the DOME. It's a popular location for schools, as radioactivity basics is considered a core (haha) curriculum piece with Math and English, so the general public is aware of the world they live in. Contrary to popular belief, there was no nuclear war pre-CORE (human era). Specific locations on Earth have extremely high levels of background radiation due to concentrated thorium or uranium in the bedrock. Radon is a naturally occurring radioactive gas produced by the decay of uranium in rock and soil. It can seep into basements and unventilated indoor spaces, occasionally reaching hazardous concentrations that make it the leading cause of lung cancer among non-smokers. There are also containers and power sources wh ich have eroded over time and begun to leak harmful chemicals into the air. To prevent further spread, they are encapsulated by these DOMES. DOMES are encased with a mirror-like coating which makes them a popular spot amongst influencers who like to record videos and do all sorts of tricks there. A vocal minority condemns these videos as they think it is 'making a spectacle of people's suffering;' There is a conspiracy that this is also done to increase the sale of proxybody rental services, as it is the only way to see inside the DOME. The leading reason seems to be so it won't disrupt the surrounding aesthetic environment. There are also theories surrounding DOMES, since some were in notorious crimminal-laden areas, or, more specifically, housed a revolution. If most of the revolutionaries have caused that much of a ruckus and live in the same area, people spectate that they are locked into the domes, and purposefully harmful chemicals are pumped in with the air supply to kill them off quietly.

It is important to realize that most of CORE has been built on top of existing structures. So the "ground" is often just sheets of metal

"Gang shit" and the curernt state of Cybercrime

Due to the current climate of online overreliance in CORE, ransomware and cybercrime gangs have generally overtaken the role of typical "gangsters". Mobs and Cartel do exist, but, work primarily physically. The lower level gangs, or lower level within crime hierarchies typically deal with cybercrime and so on. This brings about the existence of rival gangs, gang wars, cyber vandalism, an so on. Often times these disputes end up physical, which is why many members of cyber crime groups take great care to avoid their personal information being leaked. The secondary concern is for government action against them, as many cyber criminals here are either unregistered citizens or have faked their death. Hardcore gangsters spend all their time indoors with security. Constantine is a bit different since he works under a mafia hierarchy, and, has to go out on occasion because some cyber crime requires physical presence. This is why he is always with Gamma and a few others as security. Cyber criminals of course have hobbies outside of crime. Websites have overtaken the interest of music, so many cyber criminals make websites for a bunch of of random stuff and give it incredibly cool features. A recent trend has been recreating viral video games inside of a simple website.

In the early days of ransomware groups, many of them would have physical, in-person androids working as workers. They do work like active recon, updating, "regarding your previous inquiry...", news information, and so on. These androids often had access to their full database. Any technology can be cracked and this was no different. Usage of android workers became a large security vulnurability so, it's uncommon now. ⁠Constantine has the confidence to believe his android assistants won't be hacked. They probably will.

So much crime happens in the CORE universe, that the police force is robots right now. Depending on how advanced they are, they will have different heads in the game (circle heads -> square heads -> triangle heads) in level of strength/difficult to fight. So many police DIE so this is a measure done to save on medical bills and also funeral costs. They’re also pretty disposable and can be monitored and cannot be swayed.

So I didnt add it BUT if you read Constantine’s char page, he met Gamma and then basically dissected her. WHY did he do this? there is a full black market for illegally obtained mechanical parts— and even more so if they’re GI (government issued). Since so many clones die every day, there isnt really a way to like identify who is alive and who isn’t, so there’s just a hardware chip managing “is this GI?” And if it’s GI, it’s a lot easier to feign a real identity for hardware. It’s like.. an integrity check. Say I rob a spine from a police clone, and I want to put it into myself. If I were to just replace my spine with some random stolen one, I’d get flagged because “hey, this spine belongs to a dead person!/this spine doesnt match up with your medical records”. And so your location would be tracked and the whole operation would be set down. But with GI, it basically comes with those checks “pre-checked” so it says “oh, this is a GI spine. That must mean this person obtained it legally!” (This is real as most legal implants are GI) Of course Pyrrhon has measures to prevent this. When a clone is dissected, and done without extreme preventative measures, it will self destruct. This only activates when it’s dissected, not when it dies (ie falls in combat). The explosion is pretty large so it will kill whoever is dissecting it. If you were to do it say, underwater, the parts would stop working at that point. So it is VERY hard nowadays to get illegal GI parts. There is also a side business where once the parts are taken out of the robots (assuming that it didnt explode), they essentially becomes an empty shell. Since it’s incredibly good quality and lifelike, these are often repurposed and sold as sex dolls. They sell for more if the sentience/AI model is still intact, or you can input your own. So essentially defeated police bots are repurposed into prostitutes.

Now, about that side quest.. you’d find a half-dead police bot in an alleyway and would be granted the dilemma of “dissect for money” or “let her rest in peace and mitigate death risks”. It’s mostly a moral thing because you, Nea, decide whether you side with Honey/Gamma (“it’s a living being since we believe that clones are sentient entities and should be treated as such”) or with Constantine and the others (“it is a robot + we need money + people do this to sentient entities anyway + fuck you”). You can also just leave it there. If you take neither side someone else swoops it up and uses it as a sex thing and you see it later.

Astornaut Helmets

While astronaut helmets are often used as torture and investigation tools, they are sometimes used for general life continence as well. Clones with nightly OSs or illegal AIs will often sleep with an ‘astronaut helmet’, which is essentially just an air gapped LAN Every port is connected into the helmet (physical device over the head), which is just an air gapped network. You cannot access the outside internet at all, you’re just stuck there. You can acess your local things, yeah, but you can’t connect to internet, you don’t have audio modules anymore, you can’t hear anything, you can’t see anything but your own OS… it’s just you.

Illegal AI-hosters or nightly OS’ will have these because when the organic mind sleeps, it can’t keep a very tight control over what the AI wants to access and what it doesnt. So if the AI can seperate from the organic mind while sleeping and do its own thing, it creates a split entity (stand alone complex reference hahahhahahaha gits reference ahhahahahhahah) and does random shit. So they’re usually locked off during sleep. The AI is NOT the organic mind, it CO runs with the organic mind. The organic mind governs the AI. And so when that is asleep, the AI can act independently.

The helmet also process throttles so that the AI doesn’t go and rewrite internal systems. These are commonly used by illegal AI hosts, regular clones who carry these ‘just in case’ or when a suspicious patch just dropped, people with nightly or unstable OS’, placed on POWs by police, and tinfoil hat conspirators.

I won’t say that the helmet itself is great, since, it’s not very healthy for the organic mind. This is why Gamma and Honey only use astronaut helmets unless absolutely necessary

Long term effects include identity instability, false parallelization (the AI does not actually split from the organic mind BUT you think the AI has split and it causes long term stress), paranoia, and inevitable dependence on the helmet where the organic mind weakens to the point the AI ‘takes over’ (human cognitive layer has declined so much that the AI assistance becomes the dominant decision maker)

Post ‘submarine sleep’ (sleeping with astronaut helmet), people will often notice that long messages were drafted and attempted to send over and over again, files reorganized/subtly renamed, random gibberish but with occasional readable plaintext output in the thought console, and so on. It’s a fearful thing

There is a conspiracy that the gibberish with occasional readable plaintext is actually a religious entity attempting communication with the organic being, so, some particularly deranged folks just sleep with it all the time in the hopes of asencion.

I mean, predictive text systems are loooping. Auto indexing systems misfiring. Language model running without proper input constraints. The plaintext isn’t anything special, it’s just stuff in cache. Heavy users just fucking die in it.

Asylums are often filled with astronaut helmets. Constantine has stopped all usage of astronaut helmets because sometimes he walks into a closet and a bunch of rotting, deteriorating bodies spill out and the only thing that hasn’t rotted was the helmets. People die in it. It’s really bad. People die because .. they can’t eat without outside assistance. So they all either starve to death, OR (for example a POW would be placed under helmet supervision as a safety constraint, officers would forget, POW starves/overheats and dies) So asylums often use helmets as containment/treatment/control but if patients are monitored poorly and forgotten about,t hey just die. They’re removed from reality completely.

Officially in police and legal usage, helmets are ‘AI containment protocol’ but is really just used as indefinite isolation and deliberate neglect. So people die from being either: afraid to leave Being forcefully locked inside People never coming back to check on them The helmet blocks I/O so you can’t call for help, you can’t tell time, you can’t move, and so on. The helmet itself is fine. The helmet is a good device and acomplishes its goal, it’s just the usage (or, misusage) of it

Vegetables

in CORE, vegetables are typically served dehydrated and dried. The process goes: flatten vegetables (typically herbs like parsley and mints) -> extract water -> sell each separately The dried vegetables lack most of the nutritional content, which is why the extracted water is typically sold at a markup. The water is expensive and popular amongst young children and adults, for being a 'revitalizing golden liquor' (as an ad will tell you). it's kind of like a healthy Celsius in the culture. It is usually too expensive for common folks (notably Constantine) to access it but Nea, who grew up generally wealthy, drank it on the daily. It was primarily marketed as a 'nutrient on the go', as most office workers don't have time to eat or drink meals. the leading company offers package deals with offices and mass production corporations which have organic life doing manual labor, by providing small, daily packets of the water as a replacement for medication. This is done due to an act passed a while back, which stat ed that all workers must be granted access to food, water, and a healthy amount of medication and vitamins. The water is granted as all of these options, as it has calories and can be classified as a meal. Clones do not need it. Th ere are a few laws which state that certain work (ie management, it, etc) must be done by organic life.

The vegetables can be 'rehydrated' using separate, third party machinery, but will usually produce a result akin to soggy bread.

This is done because vegetables are often stored for long periods of time in transport or in stores, and also because the water provides a considerable amount of profit compared to the raw vegetables, which are only served in high end restaurants. Few farms do exist outside of corporations, and are mostl y located in temples where they are protected by religious law as to not be overtaken by a corporation. "But the dried vegetables retain most of the nutrients and the extracted water is really just water and some vitamins.." correct! it's a scam. it is artificially fortified by synthetic vitamins, electrolytes, cloned amino acids, glucose, mild stimulants, appetite suppressants, and on occasion: nicotine.

It is not sustainable and many organic-life workers end up dead after relying solely on this.

The dried vegetables have nutrients but taste like cardboard, are expensive, look ugly, and are too difficult to prepare. So they are used primarily as garnish.

the general public isn't exactly aware that the whole thing is generally a scam, since, it's a large corporation (imagine nestle). workers who rely on it have a bleaker, more cynical view. middle/upper class folks who drink it as a fun energy drink/supplement dearly love it (think dedication to stanley cups and starbucks)

... which brings me to cooking. in short, since, i'm busy play ing games, the majority of middle/upper class folks do not know how to cook and rely either on pre-prepped meals, personal chefs, daily restaurant trips, or if they are particularly cybernetic, then injecitble nutrient meals.

Being a chef is sort of like being a farmer. Neccesary but low class. Chefs are usually seen as illiterate, poor immigrants (which they usually are) who are exploited and are at near slave level with how they are treated. It is not at all a gordon ramsey flashy job... unless you're rich! There are plenty of high end restaurants, notably a single chain which I don't have a name for yet. They advertise their real vegetable use (this is true, and it is their main selling point) and natural meat (as most meat available to the public is either insects, artificially printed, or mass cloned). Lower end people (const) typically know the basics of cooking.

Netrunning ----- Since a lot of netrunning is done via personal hardware embedded in the person, constantine and many others employ Zombies to do their dirty work

So that things can't be traced back to them, they can buy zombies. These are people who have died but aren't officially registered as dead. This includes clones (safest) to real life people. It is a highly illegal market as I'm sure you can imagine.

These are basically like virtual machines but for personal machinery. Obviously people use PCs and Laptops but this is for doing more advanced hacks. Laptops can only do so much in the CORE world..

You can technically use a real alive person as a zombie but they're going to die once it's inevitably traced back. being zombified is like a death sentence.

Some particularly notorious folks rent out zombie servers (warehouses with cooled corpses). You can temporarily rent a zombie. It's good because if you're doing it for an individual project and you don't want to buy a whole zombie + do maintenance this is good.

You can also rent out full zombie warehouses and servers for advanced DDOSs. As you can also imagine the servers are raided frequently, so they are entirely remotely hosted. They are built with bombs in the zombies and also in the hardware so there's many reports of people accidentally wandering into a server warehouse and dying because the place self destructs basically.

Refined vs Unrefined Magic

Unrefined magic is the act of generating magic from the environment and the body, drawing from emotions and internal and external energy. While it can be limitless, it's very taxing. It's much more powerful and can be indestructible under the right circumstances, but you die early. thags a lie. Pyrrhon spread that as a lie (she killed the unrefined mg users bc they were a threat) and shifted public opinion to use refined magic instead. Padme is one more unrefined magic user.

Refined magic is basically what everyone under the age of 200 uses-- magic that's less taxing to the body. Instead of creating magic from nowhere and doing shit with it, it must be channeled through a magic transmuting object ( sword, a book, hammer, etc) and exerted through electricity )from the obj$ and also the user. Much less powerful, but easier to control and learn. To assist with refined magic sometimes people get crystal tattoos (nea has one on her forehead, shenx on his neck, etc) to help regulate internal magic flow + mark refined magic.

However most people get that tattoo without refined magic and it's more of a fashion statement now.

Hypothetical Politics

I've been tossing and turning but essentially it's a totalitarianist environment! I have a LOT to say about the core politics. Pyrrhon is the sole ruler of like. the whole Earth. She's legally required to do EVERYTHING but since she obviously can't, she allows other officials to do them in her name as a loophole so she really is just the world leader. Imagine a fuher

in core if/when yly kill Pyrrhon, the world falls into chaos. No rhler, no rule, no law.. Whats next? Everything has been torn apart! It brings in-- or is supposed to bring in-- the immaturity of the chars. That they just rushed in without knowledge, and .. maybe things would've been better off if Pyrrhon just stayed alive. Now there's no rulers and the world has fallen apart the game ends right as she dies, so it's up to interpretation. But the only glue holding the world together is dead.

In short it's a one world order. Imagine if we lived in Pangea. That's basically what it is. Pyrrhon, or the president or furher or emperor rules over it. CORE is a very anarchist game, but it brings in the flaws of postmodernism and anarchists in which "what other alternative do you have?" Thays my main criticism towards post modernism and modern anarchy. But tl;dr one world order
